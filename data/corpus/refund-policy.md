# Refund Policy

Customers on the Pro plan may request a refund within 30 days of purchase. Refunds
are issued to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

Annual subscriptions are eligible for a prorated refund if cancelled within the first
14 days. After 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable but remain active until the end
of the billing period.

Refunds are not available for one-time add-on purchases such as extra storage.
